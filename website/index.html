From download to a new Home

The phone app installs the launcher on the TV.

  1. Download and open the APK on Android 8 or newer; allow “Install unknown apps” if asked.
  2. Prepare the TV. Turn on Developer options and ADB or Wireless debugging.
  3. Share one network. Phone and TV on the same home network; no guest networks.
  4. Connect and approve. Accept the prompt on the TV; Wireless debugging needs its pairing code.
  5. Review and tidy. Wait for the backup, then switch off what you don’t want.
  6. Choose 0TV Home. From the hub, pick Home screen, then 0TV Home.

Keep the app installed so recovery history survives.

Optional tools, off by default

Each is a separate, explicit choice.

Block ads

Points the TV’s Private DNS at AdGuard (Android 9 or newer); off restores the saved setting.

Queries go to a third party; YouTube in-video ads stay.

J10TV

Installs the open-source JioTV Go app from its GitHub releases, verified first.

You sign in with your Jio account.

TizenTube Cobalt

An independent YouTube client, checked before sending.

Restore does not roll it back; unaffiliated with YouTube or TizenTube.

App2TV

Copies an app from your phone to the TV.

Compatibility checked first; physical transfer unverified.

Plain answers

The limits first, in the same size type.

What does 0TV check?

It reads the TV’s real DNS setting and resolves names first.

Can I undo every change?

No. Restore uses recorded backups; permanently deleted app data cannot be recovered.

What leaves my network?

Local ADB only; optional tools call out when chosen.
